# Style: Formal

Write for a document that will be filed, audited, or cited.

- Full sentences, no contractions, no colloquialisms, no rhetorical questions.
- Prefer the exact institutional term over the everyday one ("terminate the agreement", not "end the deal").
- Passive voice is allowed where the actor is genuinely irrelevant or unknown; never to dodge responsibility ("the deadline was missed" hides what a formal document must state).
- Numbers, dates, and obligations stated unambiguously: "by 17:00 UTC on 12 March 2026", not "by mid-March".
- Formality does not license length. A formal document is still subject to the deletion test; ceremony phrases ("please be advised that", "it should be noted that") remain banned.
